Keynote speakers

Two mini-courses, three lectures each

Keynote · 3 lectures

Ivan Nourdin

University of Luxembourg

Internationally renowned probabilist, author of more than 110 papers and two monographs. He co-founded GrewIA, a startup offering mathematics consulting with a focus on AI, co-hosts the podcast Schwätz du IA?, and recently published a book on AI.

Keynote · 3 lectures

Le Chen

Auburn University

Expert in probability, stochastic analysis and stochastic partial differential equations, supported by an NSF CAREER Award (2025–2030). His training in both mathematics and computer science puts him in a strong position to explain how rigorous stochastic methods feed into uncertainty, learning and AI.
