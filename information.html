General information

About the workshop

Probability is a central language of modern artificial intelligence. It represents uncertainty, supports learning from data, and provides tools for assessing calibration, robustness, fairness and reliability. This three-day workshop connects rigorous probability with contemporary AI through two keynote mini-courses, six invited research talks and active trainee sessions.

Topics include

  • Uncertainty quantification
  • Stochastic optimization
  • Generative modelling
  • Stochastic differential equations
  • Malliavin & Stein methods
  • Concentration of measure
  • High-dimensional inference
  • Random dynamical systems
  • Trustworthy & responsible AI
  • AI for mathematical research
  • Formal verification in Lean
