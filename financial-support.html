Financial support

Support for graduate students and postdocs

Graduate students and postdoctoral researchers can apply for financial support toward travel and accommodation. The support is up to a maximum of CAD $350 per person.

Apply by May 1, 2027 · Decisions by email by May 15, 2027

  1. Email the organizer

    Send an email to Raluca Balan at rbalan@uottawa.ca expressing your interest in attending and in receiving support.

  2. Ask for a reference letter

    Ask your supervisor to send a reference letter directly to Raluca Balan at rbalan@uottawa.ca.

  3. Register for the workshop

    Please also fill in the free registration form.
